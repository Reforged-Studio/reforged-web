Common questions

Why do my times say Scheduled instead of Live?

Real-time predictions from NJ TRANSIT require an approved developer application, which is pending. Until it is granted, NJ TRANSIT bus, rail and light rail times come from the timetable bundled in the app and are labelled Scheduled. PATH is already live.

The timetable is the floor, and it is always there. Live predictions sit on top of it when the agency provides them — so a Scheduled time is a real countdown, it just can't know that a particular bus is running late. StopSpotter labels it rather than letting you assume otherwise.

Why does my widget show times that look old?

iOS decides how often widgets may refresh in the background, and it budgets that across every widget on the device. StopSpotter can ask; it cannot insist.

So the widget always states how old its data is, and tapping the footer fetches fresh predictions immediately. That is the trade: a widget that is instant and occasionally stale, and honest about which. Past fifteen minutes it says so outright.

What is free, and what costs money?

The app is free, and not a limited version — search, nearby stops, as many saved stops as you like, full detail on any of them, every mode, live and scheduled times. Free also includes a Home Screen widget for whichever stop you are nearest to.

StopSpotter+ adds widgets for the stops you have saved, which is what lets you keep a morning bus and an evening train on the Home Screen at once. It is $2.99, once — not a subscription. No ads and no account either way.

What do I choose when I save a stop?

The stop itself, and then as much or as little as you want on top of it: which routes you care about, which direction, and a name of your own.

Narrowing to one or two routes keeps the list short and useful; leaving it open shows everything departing that stop. The name you give it is what appears on the widget, so “Morning bus” beats a stop code and a direction string.

How do I add a widget and choose which stop it shows?

Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, find StopSpotter, and pick a size.

Then touch and hold the widget you just placed, tap Edit Widget, and choose the saved stop. That last step is the one people miss — a freshly placed widget hasn't been pointed at anything yet. Widgets for saved stops need StopSpotter+; the free widget finds your nearest stop on its own and needs no setup.

How accurate is the gate information at Port Authority?

Terminal gate assignments are best-effort and may change. Check station signage before boarding.

Gates are shown where the data provides them, which is not everywhere and not always in advance.

Which agencies and modes are supported?

NJ TRANSIT bus, rail and light rail, plus PATH. Not New York City Transit, not Amtrak, and not private carriers.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

Not yet.
