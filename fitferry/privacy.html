Privacy policy

Your data never reaches us.

This policy describes how FitFerry handles your information. The short version: FitFerry does its work on your iPhone or iPad, and we collect nothing.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

NO SERVERS NO ACCOUNTS NO ANALYTICS NO TRACKING

The whole policy in one sentence FitFerry doesn't collect your data: nothing you import, export, inspect, or view reaches us or is sold, and the app does its work on your iPhone or iPad.

What FitFerry does with your data

FitFerry reads workout files you choose (GPX, TCX, FIT, or zip and gzip archives containing them) and, at your explicit direction, writes their contents to Apple Health on your device. It can also read workouts from Apple Health, again only when you ask it to, in order to create GPX or TCX files that you save or share yourself.

All parsing, importing, and exporting is performed locally, on your device. FitFerry has no server component. FitFerry never sends your files or your health data to us. The only part of them that ever leaves your device is a single map point, which Apple's frameworks send to Apple to look up a place name or a time zone, as described under "What goes to Apple".

Health data (HealthKit)

FitFerry uses Apple's HealthKit framework. It requests permission to read and write workout data, and it uses that access only to carry out the imports and exports you initiate. Specifically:

  • Health data is read or written only at your explicit direction, when you choose to import a file or to export workouts.
  • Health data never reaches us. The only part of it that leaves your device is the starting point of a workout's route, which Apple looks up for a place name (see "What goes to Apple").
  • Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or any similar purpose.
  • Health data is never shared with, sold to, or disclosed to third parties.

You can review or revoke FitFerry's Health permissions at any time in the Health app or in Settings, and you can delete anything FitFerry has written to Apple Health, either in FitFerry (one workout, one batch, or everything it imported) or directly from the Health app. FitFerry only ever deletes workouts it wrote itself.

What FitFerry does not do

Rather than list categories of data we might collect, it's simpler to state what is true: we don't collect any. FitFerry has:

  • No data collection of any kind. No personal information, no usage data, no identifiers.
  • No analytics and no crash-reporting services.
  • No tracking, no advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no credentials to store.
  • No servers operated by us that the app talks to.

Purchases

The one-time lifetime unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's own privacy policy. Reforged Studio never sees your payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether the unlock has been purchased, so it can enable unlimited imports.

What goes to Apple

Apple's Core Location framework sends a single map coordinate to Apple to look up a place name for a workout's starting point on the Export tab, or the time zone of a route whose times carry no local offset. FitFerry sends nothing else with it: no file, workout, heart rate, or name; Apple handles these requests under Apple's own privacy policy, and Reforged Studio never receives them. Purchases also go through Apple: StoreKit checks with the App Store for the unlock's price and whether you've bought it, as described under "Purchases".

Your files

Files you open with FitFerry are read from wherever you keep them (the Files app, iCloud Drive, or a shared location) using the standard iOS document picker and share sheet. A file you share to FitFerry from another app is copied into FitFerry so it can be read. The Import Log, a record of what FitFerry wrote to Apple Health, and the place names it has looked up are kept on your device. FitFerry does not copy your files anywhere beyond what's needed to parse them on your device, and files you export are saved or shared only to the destinations you choose.

Children

FitFerry does not collect data from anyone, including children. The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the effective date above revised before that version ships. Because the app collects nothing, we expect changes to be rare.
