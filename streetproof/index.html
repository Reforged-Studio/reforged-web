How it works

Find the red. Turn it blue.

STEP 1

Spot

Mid-run, your wrist shows the blocks you haven't claimed. Red Proof Pins mark what's left.

STEP 2

Collect

Run past a pin and it's yours. Every run ends with the new ones you picked up.

STEP 3

Complete

Every pin moves your city's number. Keep going until it reads 100%.

City progress

See how close you are to covering every block, in every city you run in. Run somewhere new and that city adds itself, in the US and a growing list of countries.
