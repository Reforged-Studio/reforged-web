One place

Your whole commute in one app.

A trip that touches a bus and a train means more than one app today — and more than one place to look even inside them, because operators and modes get filed separately. StopScout doesn't file them separately. Every operator and mode it supports lives in one index, so one search reaches all of it and mode arrives as a tag on the result rather than a choice you make first.

A bus stop and a PATH station turn up in the same search and sit side by side in Saved. You stop keeping a mental map of which app knows about which part of your journey.

Never a dead end

There is always an answer.

Real-time feeds go down. Timetables don't. StopScout carries both, so an answer is always on screen — and the footer tells you which kind you're reading and how much to trust it. You never have to open something else to find out when the next one is meant to leave.

  1. Feed reporting updated 1 min ago

    Live predictions from the agency, stamped with the moment they arrived.

  2. Feed lagging updated 18 min ago — may not reflect real-time data

    Still the last real prediction, but old enough that StopScout stops vouching for it. One tap fetches again.

  3. Feed unavailable Schedule data

    Timetable times, bundled in the app and needing no network at all — so you still learn when the next one is meant to leave.

The labelling isn't a disclaimer. It's the difference between a number you can plan around and a number worth double-checking.

Look up any stop. Free.

Not a widget with an app bolted on. Open StopScout and you can search any stop in the system, see everything upcoming there, check what's near you, and save as many stops and routes as you want — none of which costs anything.

Placing one

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, choose StopScout and a size. Then touch and hold the widget you just placed, tap Edit Widget, and pick the saved stop it should show — that last step is the one people miss.

What it costs

$2.99, one time

Not a subscription. Bought through the App Store, restorable on your other devices.

iOS budgets background widget refreshes per app rather than per widget, so the more you place the less often each one updates on its own. The tap is always there, and every widget says how old its data is.
