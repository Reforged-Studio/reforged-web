Common questions

Why do my bus times say Scheduled instead of Live?

Real-time predictions from NJ TRANSIT require an approved developer application, which is pending. Until it is granted, bus and rail departures come from the timetable bundled in the app and are labelled Scheduled. PATH is already live.

The timetable is the floor, and it is always there. Live predictions sit on top of it when the agency provides them — so a Scheduled time is a real countdown, it just can't know that a particular bus is running late. StopScout labels it rather than letting you assume otherwise.

Why does my widget show times that look old?

iOS decides how often widgets may refresh in the background, and it budgets that across every widget on the device. StopScout can ask; it cannot insist.

So the widget always states how old its data is, and tapping the footer fetches fresh predictions immediately. That is the trade: a widget that is instant and occasionally stale, and honest about which. Past fifteen minutes it says so outright.

What is free, and what costs money?

The app is free, and not a limited version — search, nearest stops, unlimited saved combos, full stop detail, every mode, live and scheduled times. Free also includes a Home Screen widget for whichever stop you are nearest to.

StopScout+ adds widgets for the stops you have saved, which is what lets you keep a morning bus and an evening train on the Home Screen at once. It is $2.99, once — not a subscription. No ads and no account either way.

What is a combo?

A saved bundle: one stop, optionally the routes you care about, optionally a direction, and a nickname you choose. “Morning bus” is a combo. Each widget shows one.

How do I add a widget and choose which stop it shows?

Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, find StopScout, and pick a size.

Then touch and hold the widget you just placed, tap Edit Widget, and choose the saved stop. That last step is the one people miss — a freshly placed widget hasn't been pointed at anything yet. Widgets for saved stops need StopScout+; the free widget finds your nearest stop on its own and needs no setup.

Why doesn't the app icon switch to its dark version in dark mode?

That is an iOS setting rather than something the app controls. Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Customize, and choose Dark or Auto. The default pins every icon on the device to its light version, which is why it affects every app you own, not just this one.

Why does a stop say drop-off only?

Some stops in the timetable let riders off but do not take them on. StopScout labels those instead of offering a departure you could not board.

How accurate is the gate information at Port Authority?

Terminal gate assignments are best-effort and may change. Check station signage before boarding.

Gates are shown where the data provides them, which is not everywhere and not always in advance.

Which agencies and modes are supported?

NJ TRANSIT bus, rail and light rail, plus PATH. Not New York City Transit, not Amtrak, and not private carriers.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

Not yet.

Report bad data

Stop names, destination strings, gate assignments and timetables come from agency data. Some errors can be corrected in the app; others have to be reported upstream. Either way, send what you saw and where you saw it — the stop, the route, what StopScout showed and what it should have said. A screenshot helps.

Data availability is not guaranteed. When a feed is unreachable, StopScout falls back to timetable times and says so.
