Privacy policy

It doesn't collect anything.

No accounts, no analytics, no server. Saved stops stay on the device, and your location never leaves it.

Last updated 25 July 2026 · Applies to StopScout 1.0

  • No accounts
  • No analytics
  • No tracking
  • No ads
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No server
The whole policy in one sentence

StopScout has no user accounts and no server of its own; saved stops, nicknames and preferences are stored only on your device, and your location is used on-device only, to find stops near you.

What StopScout stores, and where

Saved combos, nicknames and preferences are written to storage on your device and stay there. There is no cloud sync and no account, so there is no copy anywhere else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location

If you allow it, StopScout uses your location to list stops near you, and to point the nearest-stop widget at the right stop. That lookup runs against a transit database bundled inside the app, on the device. Your coordinates are not transmitted to Reforged Studio or to anyone else, and they are not stored between sessions.

You can change or revoke this at any time in Settings › Apps › StopScout. Without it, everything except nearby search and the nearest-stop widget still works — you can search for stops by name.

Network requests

To show times, StopScout requests predictions directly from NJ TRANSIT's public developer feeds and from a community-hosted PATH real-time feed. Those requests go from your device to those third parties, who necessarily see standard network information such as your IP address, subject to their own practices.

Those requests do not pass through any server operated by Reforged Studio, and Reforged Studio receives nothing from them. The requests contain a stop identifier — never your location, and never any identifier for you or your device beyond what any network request carries.

Schedule data

Timetables, route names and stop locations ship inside the app and update when the app updates. Reading them involves no network request at all.

Purchases

StopScout+ is purchased through the App Store. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple, under Apple's own privacy policy. Reforged Studio never sees your payment details.

Children

StopScout collects no data from anyone, including children. It is a general-purpose transit utility and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the date above revised before that version ships.

Contact
